# Ostrava 90's Brawl

### Toňo wants to sleep. The city disagrees.

**[Download](releases/Ostrava90sBrawl-1.0.apk)** · Android 8.0+ · offline · no account

---

Mid-90s. Ostrava. Smog, neon, tower blocks. Law is a word someone heard on television.

Toňo, a miner. A few beers on Stodolní. He wants to get home to Hlubina and sleep before the shift.

He is not a hero. He is tired.

The street does not care. One night. Five streets. Punch, kick, jump, dodge. A weapon off the ground when you walk through it. Someone stronger at the end of every street.

Then a hallway. Two police. All night someone reported a man beating people across the city. Toňo says he wanted to go to sleep.

He slept before the shift. Just not at home.

---

A brawl in landscape. A walk home, not revenge. Czech or English. The game stays on the phone. No account. No online.

---

For anyone who likes arcade fighting and cities that smell of diesel and beer. For anyone who does not want to play the chosen one.

Toňo is not the Batman of Ostrava. He wants to sleep.
