Mid-90s. Ostrava. Smog, neon, tower blocks. Law is a word someone heard on television.

Toňo, a miner. A few beers on Stodolní. He wants to get home to Hlubina and sleep before the shift. He is not a hero. He is tired.

One night. Five streets. Punch, kick, jump, dodge. Then a hallway. Two police. He slept before the shift. Just not at home.

A brawl in landscape. A walk home, not revenge. Czech or English. No account. No online. Toňo is not the Batman of Ostrava. He wants to sleep.
